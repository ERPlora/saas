  Wrapper of the allauth mails that have no template of their own (password reset, «Google was
  linked»…), ported from ERPlora/saas. allauth's own says «Hello from {{ site_name }}!», and that
  name comes from the `Site` row («example.com»). The brand is written here and does NOT depend on
  `Site`: a misconfigured deployment may break a link, but must not make the mail look like
  phishing.
