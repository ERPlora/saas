# Use ERPlora on your devices

ERPlora is a web app: your Hub opens in a browser and its database lives in the cloud managed by ERPlora. There is nothing else to install to use the ERP. There is a single installable **ERPlora app** for desktop and Android — the same Hub, packaged — and it is what reaches point-of-sale hardware.

## The Hub in a browser

The Hub runs in a current desktop or mobile browser and requires an internet connection. Open your business's address, or open it from erplora.com, and sign in as described in **First access and navigation**.

For the best experience, keep the browser updated and allow the Hub to store site data. Private browsing and automatic site-data deletion make the browser stop being a device of the business: you will have to sign in once more with your erplora.com account.

## Keep the Hub on your phone's home screen

At the foot of the side menu, the "Open on your phone" QR code opens the business on the phone. To keep it there, add it to your home screen from your browser menu. It is the same Hub: the first time you sign in with your erplora.com account, as on any new device.

## Hardware: the installed app

To print thermal receipts or open the cash drawer, install the **ERPlora app** on the device next to the printer: on its network, with its USB cable or paired over Bluetooth. A browser cannot reach the printers. There is no separate program: it is the same Hub.

Then, in the Printing app (from **Settings › Printing**, "Printers and ticket"), add the printer under "Printers" and, from that same device, tap "Print from this device" under "Devices". See [the ERPlora app](/app/) for the supported hardware.

## Using several devices

Several devices can work in the same business, and they all share its database in the cloud, so there is nothing to copy or synchronize between them. On each one, somebody has to sign in once with their account. The plan says how many devices can have a session open at the same time: entering on one more closes the session of the one that was entered longest ago.
