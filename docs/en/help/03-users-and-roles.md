# Users, roles and PIN access

In ERPlora, the people of a business are those of erplora.com:

- The **erplora.com account** belongs to each person: they sign in with their email and password, with Google or with the verification code they get by email. With it they enter every business they have been invited to.
- Inside each business, the person has a **role** ("Owner", "Admin", "Manager" or "Employee") and, if they want, a **PIN** and a **badge** to enter at shared tills.

## Invite, change the role of and remove a person

People are invited, have their role changed and are removed on erplora.com, in the business's "Members". From the Hub you get there with:

- "Invite on erplora.com", in **Employees › Staff**;
- "Change roles on erplora.com", in **Employees › Roles**;
- "Open on erplora.com", in a person's card.

The invitation arrives by email and, once the person accepts it, they show up in **Employees › Staff** with their role. Whoever is removed from the business can no longer enter. To bring them back, invite them again: their PIN and badge still work. Above the **Employees › Staff** table you see how many of the plan's seats are taken.

## How each person enters

In **Employees › Staff**, the "Access" column says how each one enters: "Account" always and, if they have them, "PIN" and "Badge". Whoever runs the business taps a row to open the person's card, with "Their details", "PIN" and "Badge":

- **PIN**: the person chooses it, the first time they enter at a till or in **My profile**. From the card it can only be removed, with "Remove the PIN"; the person still enters with their account.
- **Badge**: pass the card over the reader (or type its number) and tap "Save badge". Nobody sets up their own badge.

A card in the Staff app does not give access to the Hub: whoever has to enter needs to be invited on erplora.com. A role of that app ("stylist", "waiter") is a label and gives no permissions.

## How PIN access works

The PIN is a quick way to enter at a shared till, not the first sign-in on a device:

- A device only offers the PIN after somebody has signed in on it once with their account.
- In **Settings › General**, "This device" says whether the device is "Shared" (it asks for a PIN to enter and forgets it at the end of the shift) or "Personal" (the session stays open and it never asks for a PIN).
- In **Settings › General**, "PIN pad", the business decides whether the tills ask who is there ("Show PIN pad"), how many digits the PIN has ("4 digits" or "6 digits") and whether it asks again after some minutes without touching the screen ("Ask again after inactivity").
- Each person changes their PIN in **My profile**, on the "PIN" card. Two active people cannot have the same one, and easy-to-guess ones (1111, 1234) are refused. Never share your PIN.
- On a shared till, "Switch user", in the user menu, lets another person enter with their PIN or badge without losing the open sale.

## Roles and permissions

**Employees › Roles** lists the business's four roles, with how many people have each one and how many permissions it gives. The role is given when inviting and changed on erplora.com; what the person can do changes at once.

Give each person the role their job needs, no more. Only the owner and the admins change the business's settings, apps, devices and API keys.

## If someone cannot sign in

- **The PIN pad does not show:** the device is personal, the business does not show the PIN pad or nobody has signed in on it with their account yet. Enter with the "Account" tab.
- **"This device does not work with a PIN yet":** nobody has signed in here with their account, or an admin removed the device. Sign in once with your account and from then on it will work.
- **"Wrong PIN.":** check that you chose your name. After several wrong PINs in a row, "Too many wrong attempts." shows with the minutes to wait. If you do not remember your PIN, ask whoever runs the business to remove it and choose a new one in **My profile**.
- **"Session opened on another device":** the plan covers a number of devices at a time and, when somebody entered on another one, this one's session was closed. Sign in again or upgrade the plan.
- **A screen or an app is missing:** it depends on the person's role and on the apps the business has turned on.
- **The person is no longer in Staff:** they were removed from the business on erplora.com; invite them again with "Invite on erplora.com".
