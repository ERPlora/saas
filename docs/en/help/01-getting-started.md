# First access and navigation

ERPlora is a modular business management system. Your Hub contains the tools, users and data for one business, while the Cloud account identifies who is allowed to access it.

## Sign in for the first time

On a new or untrusted device:

1. Open your Hub URL or the ERPlora application.
2. Sign in with the email address and password of your ERPlora Cloud account.
3. Enable **Trust this device** if it is a private work device.
4. Choose and confirm a **4-digit PIN** when prompted.
5. ERPlora opens the Hub and creates a local session with the permissions assigned to your user.

> PIN access is only available after the device has been trusted. On a new device, or after clearing the browser's site data, sign in with email and password again.

## Return with your PIN

On a trusted device:

1. Select your user if more than one person uses the device.
2. Enter your 4-digit PIN.
3. Use the **Email** tab whenever you need to sign in with Cloud credentials instead.

The PIN belongs to a local Hub user. It does not replace the email and password of the Cloud account.

## There is no general setup wizard

ERPlora does not run a multi-step setup wizard after login. Configuration is contextual:

- The dashboard shows a notice when an installed app requires configuration.
- An administrator can open the notice and complete that app's settings.
- Business identity, language, currency, permissions and data tools are managed from **Settings**.
- Additional capabilities are installed from **Apps**.

## Find your way around the Hub

- **Home** — widgets from installed apps, recent activity and terminal status.
- **Employees** — Hub users, roles and permissions.
- **Files** — files and attachments stored by the Hub and its apps.
- **Billing** — plan and billing information for the Hub.
- **Apps** — installed apps and the app catalog.
- **System** — runtime, storage, connection and diagnostic information.
- **Settings** — personal preferences and Hub-wide configuration.

The top bar also provides the **app launcher**, the ERPlora **Assistant** and notifications. Inside an app, its own sections appear in the app's tab bar.

## Recommended first checks

If you are the administrator:

1. Open **Settings → Hub** and confirm language, currency and appearance.
2. Open **Settings → Business** and complete the legal and fiscal identity used by invoices.
3. Review the dashboard for apps that still need configuration.
4. Open **Apps** to confirm which apps are installed and available for your plan.
