# First access and navigation

ERPlora is a modular management system for your business. Each business has its own Hub, with its apps, its team and its data, while your **erplora.com account** says who you are and which businesses you can enter.

## Sign in for the first time

1. Open your business's address or the ERPlora app. You can also sign in on erplora.com and open your business from there: you enter without typing your password again.
2. On a device that does not offer a PIN you go straight to the erplora.com sign-in. On a till that does, **Access** shows up: tap the "Account" tab and then "Enter with your account".
3. On erplora.com, sign in with your email and password, with Google or with the verification code you get by email.
4. You come back to the business already inside, on **Home** or on the screen you had asked for.

There is no box to tick: when you sign in with your account, that browser becomes a trusted device of the business. It shows up in **Settings › General**, under "Devices", and starts as a shared device.

On a shared till of a business that asks for a PIN, if you do not have one yet, "Create your PIN" shows up before you enter: choose a PIN with the number of digits the business uses (4 or 6) and repeat it. "Not now" leaves it for later; you can set it whenever you like in **My profile**.

## Return with your PIN

On a shared till or tablet of a business that asks for a PIN, **Access** opens on the "PIN" tab:

1. Under "Choose your user", tap your name. If there is only one person, it is already selected.
2. Type your PIN: you enter on the last digit, with no button.
3. You can also pass your badge over the reader, without choosing your name first.

The PIN is yours inside this business and does not replace your erplora.com account: the "Account" tab always lets you sign in with it. On a personal device the session stays open and it never asks for a PIN.

## What is left to set up

ERPlora has no multi-step setup wizard. What is pending shows on **Home**, under "Finish setting up your business":

- How many steps you have done and the ones left: first those marked "Needed to invoice", then those marked "Important".
- Each step has "Set up", which opens the screen where it is done.
- While a "Needed to invoice" step is missing, the "You cannot issue invoices yet" strip shows above every other screen.
- When nothing is left, it says "Your business is ready".

Some steps can only be set up by whoever runs the business. With "Ask the assistant", the **Assistant** tells you what is missing and where it is done.

## Find your way around the Hub

The side menu (on a phone, from the button in the bar) has the platform's entries, and each person sees only the ones they can open:

- **Home** — "Summary", with "My apps", "Finish setting up your business" and the apps' panels, and "Activity", with the latest sales.
- **Employees** — "Staff", "Roles" and, for whoever runs the business, "API keys" and "Approvals".
- **Files** — the business's folders and files.
- **Apps** — "My apps", "Add apps" and, if an app is sold separately, "Paid".
- **System** — for whoever runs the business: "Usage and plan", "Updates", "Failed events" and "Logs".
- **API** — only while the business shows its API documentation.
- **Settings** — "General", "Business", "Printing" and "Data and backups".

At the foot of the menu are the QR code to open the business on your phone, "Upgrade plan" and "Manage on erplora.com". The plan and its payments are managed on erplora.com; in the Hub, **System › Usage and plan** shows how much of your plan you are using.

At the top right, "My apps" opens any app of the business, the sparkles open the **Assistant** and the bell shows the "Notifications"; on a phone, the Assistant and the bell are inside "More options". Tapping your name, at the top of the side menu, opens the user menu: "Profile", "Switch user" (on a shared till that asks for a PIN), "My account", "Report a problem", "Theme" and "Sign out". Inside an app, its sections show in its own tab bar.

## Recommended first checks

If you run the business:

1. On **Home**, follow "Finish setting up your business", starting with what is "Needed to invoice".
2. In **Settings › Business**, check the business's name, country, time zone, currency and language. The name, the time zone and the language are changed on erplora.com with "Change on erplora.com"; the currency is the country's. The fiscal data of the invoices is written in "Fiscal data", which opens the Invoicing app.
3. In **Settings › General**, decide whether this device is shared or personal and whether the business asks for a PIN at its tills.
4. In **Apps**, review "My apps" and turn on what you need in "Add apps".
5. In **Employees**, "Invite on erplora.com" takes you to the business's "Members", where the team is invited.
